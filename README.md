## 📊 Customer Analytics Projects

### Customer Churn Prediction (KNN)
- **Problem:** Customer churn reduces revenue and increases customer acquisition costs.
- **Approach:** Built a K-Nearest Neighbors (KNN) classification model using customer demographics, billing, and service usage data.
- **Key Insights:**
  - Customers with higher monthly charges showed higher churn rates.
  - Contract type and tenure were strong indicators of retention.
- **Impact:** Enables businesses to proactively target high-risk customers and improve retention strategies.

---

### Customer Segmentation (K-Means Clustering)
- **Problem:** Businesses need to understand different customer groups to personalize marketing and services.
- **Approach:** Applied K-Means clustering to segment customers based on demographic and behavioral data.
- **Key Insights:**
  - Identified distinct customer segments with varying usage patterns and spending behaviors.
- **Impact:** Supports targeted marketing strategies and improved customer engagement.

---

### Market Basket Analysis
- **Problem:** Understanding product purchase patterns can improve cross-selling and upselling strategies.
- **Approach:** Analyzed item combinations frequently purchased together using association techniques.
- **Key Insights:**
  - Certain product combinations were strongly associated with higher customer retention.
- **Impact:** Helps businesses design better product bundles and promotions.
